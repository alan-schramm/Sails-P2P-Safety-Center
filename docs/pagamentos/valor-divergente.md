# Partial payments and amount mismatches

**Scenario:** the received amount is lower or higher than agreed, or the counterparty says they completed payment through several transfers. Resolve the mismatch before treating the trade as complete.

## From the agreement to the verified difference

<SafetyFlow>

1. **Agreement** Identify the agreed amount, currency and fee treatment.
2. **Credits** Verify each separate credit with your bank or provider.
3. **Difference** Add confirmed credits once and compare with the agreement.
4. **Resolution** Record any mismatch and use the available official process. A matching total alone does not verify every other condition.

</SafetyFlow>

## Warning signs

- Requests to release the full asset amount after a partial payment.
- Receipts that do not match separate credits in your own account.
- An overpayment followed by a refund request to a different account.
- Pressure to change the price, recipient or conditions after payment.

An amount mismatch may be a mistake. It calls for verification, not an automatic accusation of fraud.

## What to verify

1. Check the recorded terms: amount, currency, recipient and any agreed fee treatment.
2. Verify each credit directly with your own bank or provider, rather than relying on receipts alone.
3. Match identifiers, amounts and times. Record each payment separately and do not count one credit twice.
4. Record the confirmed total and the difference. Do not assume a fee explains a shortfall without checking the agreement.

**Fictional example:** a trade requires BRL 1,000 and you identify two separate credits of BRL 400 and BRL 350. The verified total is BRL 750; another receipt image does not confirm the remaining BRL 250.

## Address the difference

Record the discrepancy in the trade's authenticated channel. Preserve the original terms and replies; do not edit the agreement to make the payment appear correct. If it remains unresolved, use the application's available support or dispute process.

Do not improvise a proportional crypto release: confirm whether the actual workflow supports it and which authorizations it requires. Do not send another payment to an account supplied in a message as a condition for resolving the earlier one.

For a PIX refund, ask your bank about the refund function linked to the original transaction. Brazil's Central Bank recommends that function for returning a PIX received by mistake, rather than making a new transfer to a key supplied in a message. This is Brazil-specific guidance, not a rule for all payment systems.

## Limits and privacy

This guide does not establish deadlines, cancellation rights, refund obligations or dispute outcomes. Rules depend on the method, application and jurisdiction. Share complete receipts only with authorized recipients when necessary for the case.

**Claim status:** general educational guidance; not a claim of automated reconciliation, guaranteed refunds or an implemented partial-release feature.
