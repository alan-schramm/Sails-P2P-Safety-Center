# Check asset, network, address and fees

**Scenario:** you are about to send crypto. An asset name or apparently correct address alone does not establish that the recipient accepts payment on your selected network.

## Before confirming

1. Verify the exact asset, sending network and network accepted at the destination using authenticated trade and receiving-service instructions.
2. Compare the full address on the confirmation screen. Do not rely only on its beginning and end or on changed instructions in a message.
3. If the destination requires a memo, tag or other reference, verify it in official instructions. Do not invent a value or assume every destination requires the same field.
4. Review the amount sent, fees and expected amount at the destination. Check sufficient balance for the wallet's required fees.
5. Check the service's limits and confirmation requirements. Do not apply one universal confirmation count to all networks and trades.

## After sending

Keep the transaction reference and check its status on the corresponding network. Network confirmation and the credit shown by a service are different checks. If the destination does not show the funds, seek clarification before sending again.

## If something is wrong

Stop new transfers and consult official wallet or service support. Do not give recovery phrases to supposed recovery agents. Correction depends on the network, destination and control of keys; recovery is not guaranteed.

**Claim status:** general educational checklist, not a list of Sails-supported networks or a guarantee of automatic validation. Verify fee and confirmation rules with your actual wallet and provider.
