# Privacy when sharing dispute evidence

**Scenario:** you need to submit messages or receipts in a dispute, but the files also contain personal details, bank information or unrelated trades.

## Before sending

Verify who is requesting the material, through which channel and for what purpose. Knowing trade details does not prove that a message came from support. Open support through a known entry point and verify the request in the service itself.

Question requests for full account statements, identity documents or complete chat exports without an explanation of need. Never submit a recovery phrase, private key, password or authentication code as evidence.

## Preserve the original and prepare a copy

1. Keep the original file without cropping, overwriting or changing its contents, in a location with restricted access.
2. Identify which trade and discrepancy the document helps explain.
3. Prepare a sharing copy where the authorized recipient accepts one. Hide balances, unrelated transactions and unnecessary personal information.
4. Keep information needed to verify the payment or understand the sequence. If unsure, ask support which fields must remain visible and which secure channel is available.
5. Label the copy as redacted and record when, to whom and through which channel it was sent.

**Fictional example:** a statement shows the disputed payment alongside unrelated purchases. A copy can hide those purchases while keeping the fields needed to verify the transaction. Preserve the original separately.

## Check the redaction

A box drawn over text in a PDF or document may leave the underlying content selectable or recoverable. Use an appropriate redaction function on the copy and inspect the exported file. Also check the filename, comments, additional pages and location information that may accompany images.

Avoid public links and open folders. Restrict recipients and permissions where the service provides these controls. Do not send evidence to groups, social networks or AI tools without a need and an understanding of how they handle the data.

## Retention and limits

Do not delete originals while they remain necessary for the procedure or applicable preservation requirements. Afterwards, follow relevant retention and access rules; this guide sets no universal period. Ask the service who can access files and which storage and deletion policies apply.

**Claim status:** general educational guidance; no guarantee of admissibility, absolute confidentiality, encryption, deletion deadlines or an implemented Sails evidence-storage service.
