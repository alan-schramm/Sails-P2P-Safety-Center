# Pressure and threats over trade ratings

**Scenario:** a counterparty threatens to damage your reputation unless you release assets, cancel a trade or accept terms different from those recorded.

## A complaint or coercion?

Someone may disagree with service or leave a critical review. Coercion ties the rating to bypassing checks or changing terms: “release now or I will ruin your profile”. A threat does not establish that payment arrived or that the requested action is legitimate.

## Warning signs

- Demands for a positive rating before verification is complete.
- Threats of coordinated negative ratings from other accounts.
- Requests to cancel with a false reason to avoid recording a discrepancy.
- Offers to remove a review in exchange for money, release or silence.

## What to do

1. Apply the same payment and agreement checks regardless of the threat.
2. Respond factually, recording the discrepancy and what remains unverified. Avoid arguments or threats of retaliation.
3. Preserve messages before and after the demand, with timestamps and the trade identifier. An isolated crop can omit context.
4. Report the pressure through official channels and ask about rating review or dispute options, where available.
5. Do not close or cancel a trade using a reason you know is false merely to satisfy the counterparty.

**Fictional example:** after sending a receipt image, someone demands immediate release and threatens a negative review. Verify the credit with your bank and record the pressure; the threat does not replace payment confirmation.

## Leaving your own review

Describe observable facts and distinguish suspicion from confirmation. Do not publish identity documents, account numbers, phone numbers or personal details to expose someone. Do not coordinate retaliatory reviews or offer benefits for positive ratings.

## Limits

This guide cannot guarantee review removal, sanctions or protection of your profile. Applications have different rules and tools. Reputation reflects history; it does not prove the safety of a specific payment.
