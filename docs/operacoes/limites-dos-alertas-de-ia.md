# Understanding AI warnings

**Scenario:** an application displays an AI recommendation or warning during a trade. Use it to investigate facts, not as payment authorization or proof of fraud.

## What a warning means

A warning can identify a suspicious pattern and can also be wrong. No warning does not establish safety: the feature may be disabled, unavailable or unable to recognize the case. Verify trade details, payment and the channel independently of the recommendation.

## Use recommendations carefully

1. Check which information was analyzed and what the recommendation actually says.
2. Verify facts independently, using your bank and authenticated trade instructions.
3. Review amounts, recipients and conditions in every draft before authorizing an action.
4. Preserve relevant recommendations and context for a dispute without exposing unnecessary personal information.

Never send passwords, private keys, recovery phrases or authentication codes to an AI tool.

## Sails context

The consulted documentation describes drafts and advice reviewed by users; social-engineering warnings depend on operator activation. That does not establish availability in the application you use. Check which features are actually enabled.

Inference on a node does not necessarily mean execution on your phone or computer. Before submitting data, check who operates the service and its access and retention policies. This portal's AI reading tools also do not verify payments or decide disputes.

## Limits

A recommendation does not replace verification or guarantee an outcome. This article announces no autonomous agent, universal fraud detection or automatic release.
