# Pre-trade safety checklist

Before accepting a P2P trade, check the specific counterparty, payment method, instructions, and amount. A familiar trader or a high rating is not enough.

## Operational checklist
1. Confirm the asset, amount, network, rate, fees, and settlement deadline.
2. Check the agreed fiat beneficiary and account details in the authorized trade interface.
3. Review any available counterparty history and payment-account signals as risk indicators, not guarantees.
4. Verify the actual escrow and dispute terms for the selected application and settlement rail.
5. Use only approved channels; never share recovery phrases or authentication secrets.

## Stop and verify
If the recipient changes, a third party intervenes, or someone insists on moving off-platform, pause and verify before paying.

## Limits
This checklist cannot establish bank-account ownership, prevent reversals, or guarantee that a trade will complete.

**Claim status: general educational guidance; not a claim of an implemented safeguard.**
