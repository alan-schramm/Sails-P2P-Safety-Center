# Incident response during a P2P trade

When a trade shows conflicting payment information, account compromise, suspected fraud, or a disputed settlement, use a consistent response rather than improvising under pressure.

## Operational checklist
1. Pause any voluntary payment or asset-release action that has not yet been completed.
2. Verify account access and payment status through trusted official channels.
3. Preserve trade identifiers, relevant messages, receipts, and timestamps without sharing secrets.
4. Notify the relevant bank, wallet provider, or application's authorized support or dispute process as appropriate.
5. Document the response and only resume when the actual risks and permitted next steps are understood.

## Stop and verify
Avoid private-message 'recovery agents', unverified refund destinations, and promises that extra payments will unlock funds.

## Limits
This guide is not an emergency response service. Recovery, reversal, dispute windows, and legal duties depend on the providers and jurisdiction.
