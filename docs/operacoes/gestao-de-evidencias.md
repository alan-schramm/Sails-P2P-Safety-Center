# Evidence handling for professional P2P traders

Good evidence management preserves a reliable record of what was agreed and what happened, while minimizing exposure of sensitive personal and financial data.

## Operational checklist
1. Record the trade identifier, agreed terms, relevant timestamps, and authorized payment instructions.
2. Retain original bank transaction references and messages where permitted; distinguish originals from edited screenshots.
3. Record when a discrepancy was discovered and which official channel received the report.
4. Restrict access to evidence and redact unnecessary personal data before authorized sharing.
5. Follow the application's dispute rules and applicable retention and privacy requirements.

## Stop and verify
Do not alter originals to make them more persuasive, publicly accuse a counterparty, or disclose credentials.

## Limits
Evidence quality and admissibility vary by application and jurisdiction. AI analysis cannot independently certify that a document is authentic.
